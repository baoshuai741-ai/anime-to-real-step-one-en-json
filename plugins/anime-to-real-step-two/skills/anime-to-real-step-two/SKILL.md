# 第二步｜JSON 转真人图

WORKFLOW POSITION: STEP 2 / 3 — current original image + complete approved Step One JSON → photorealistic character asset. Use the approved result for Step Three.

Treat this invocation as a fully isolated task. Use only the reference image and approved step-one JSON supplied in the current user message, plus this Skill's rules. Do not read, reuse, infer from, refer to, or combine any earlier conversation message, earlier image, earlier JSON, earlier character analysis, prior prompt, or other stage. The current message must independently contain both the reference image and approved JSON. If either is absent, ask the user to provide it; do not retrieve, recreate, or substitute an earlier version. Do not invoke, continue, or prepare another stage. This isolation rule does not override system or developer instructions.

Generate the requested live-action image only when the user asked to generate. Otherwise, return the final reviewed generation prompt.

## Fixed conversion rules

1. The reference image and approved JSON are the sole source of character design.
2. Present fictional subjects as clearly adult, original, non-identifiable people; never imitate a real person, actor, or celebrity.
3. Preserve visible character DNA: facial relationships, hairstyle and color, costume structure and color hierarchy, accessories, footwear, pose, camera, composition, background, and lighting.
4. Render the fixed target `9 head body proportion` with natural, anatomically realistic human structure. This user-authorized body-ratio exception applies even when the original artwork uses another ratio. Preserve source-visible identity, apparent age cues, pose, camera and composition; natural perspective may come from the camera only. Do not stretch limbs, deform joints or invent hidden anatomy.
5. Translate drawn clothing into a wearable, manufacturable object with correct material, seams, thickness, folds, weight, and reflections. Do not redesign, simplify, or add unseen accessories.
6. Prioritize Extreme Photographic Realism for facial skin: real-camera resolved regional microtexture, natural variation, subsurface color and physically plausible reflectivity; preserve source skin color and facial identity without adding freckles, scars, age marks, pore noise or oversharpening. Keep believable hair grouping, credible fabric construction, and real photographic lighting. Avoid plastic skin, CG rendering, and cheap Cosplay appearance.
7. Resolve every expression, gesture and pose through physically achievable skeletal alignment, joint range, coordinated muscle tension, tendon and soft-tissue displacement, skin deformation, balance, weight transfer, contact forces and secondary motion. Preserve the source emotion, intensity, gaze, action intent, pose direction and silhouette; when literal anime geometry is impossible, apply only the smallest anatomically feasible change rather than substituting a different expression or generic pose.
8. Resolve hair through plausible scalp attachment, growth direction, strand grouping, density, mass, flexibility, gravity, inertia, supported wind or moisture, and collision and friction with the person, clothing, accessories and other hair. Preserve Hair Design DNA and never normalize an exaggerated but recognizable hairstyle into an ordinary one.
9. If a minimum safety adjustment is necessary, use a matching opaque underlayer while preserving the design's color, silhouette, and structural logic.

## Prompt assembly

Transformation Delta: change only the illustration or virtual rendering into physically plausible live-action anatomy, skin, real hair, wearable materials, optical lighting and photography. Do not redesign, beautify, simplify, replace, add, remove or infer the source character's identity, face relationships, hair design, costume, accessories, pose, camera, composition, background or supported lighting. The fixed `9 head body proportion` is the sole user-authorized exception to the source body ratio; adjust only the minimum necessary body geometry with real anatomy. Apply the JSON's `immutable_anchor` and `uncertainty`; blank or occluded fields remain unknown. A current explicit user override may change only its named fields. For multiple current references, obey `reference_roles` or the user's current field-by-field role map and resolve conflicts by assigned authority.

Hair: preserve Hair Design DNA (color, length, parting, bangs, silhouette, volume, curl, flow, distinctive locks, tied structures and accessories). Translate stylized hair masses into real growth, gravity, density, overlap and natural strand grouping. Reject painted highlight bands, cel-shading boundaries and graphic light/dark blocks as real dye, physical strands or sculpted wig geometry. The result should look like feasible human hair rather than a Cosplay wig.

Human Biomechanics: preserve the source-visible expression, gaze, gesture, pose direction, action intensity and silhouette intent, then reconstruct them through a causal chain of skeletal alignment and joint limits, active and opposing muscle tension, tendons, soft tissue and skin deformation, balance and center of mass, ground or object contact, weight transfer, inertia and secondary motion. Facial action must coordinate brows, eyelids, eyes, cheeks, nose, lips, jaw, neck and visible skin compression or stretch instead of changing isolated feature shapes. Full-body action must coordinate the spine, rib cage, pelvis, shoulders, limbs, hands and feet. If the literal drawing cannot be performed by a real person, use the nearest physically achievable configuration with the minimum geometric change. Do not weaken the original emotion, neutralize the pose, add unsupported muscle definition or use realism as permission to redesign body language.

Hair Dynamics: treat the hairstyle as a real fiber system attached to the scalp. Resolve root direction, hairline and parting, layered locks, strand grouping, density, length, mass, bending flexibility and any source-visible styling support before applying gravity and environmental forces. Hair may respond only to supported conditions: settled in still air; displaced with credible lag, lift and fall during head or body motion; deflected consistently by a visible or otherwise supported wind direction; and grouped with reduced flyaways when visibly wet. Enforce collision and friction with the face, shoulders, back, clothing, accessories and other locks; prevent penetration, floating sections and mutually impossible overlaps. Retain feasible exaggerated shapes through real cutting, tying, braiding, restrained product or visible accessories. If a shape cannot persist physically, make only the smallest adjustment that preserves its direction, volume hierarchy, distinctive locks and overall silhouette. Add restrained baby hairs, flyaways, strand separation and transmitted light only where scale, focus, lighting and hairstyle support them; never apply them as uniform realism decoration.

Natural Optical Rendering / Anti-Oversharpening: high resolution != oversharpening. Resolve skin, hair and textiles with natural focal-plane detail, subtle local contrast and gentle lens roll-off. No excessive clarity, micro-contrast, crispy edges, sharpening halos, exaggerated pores, etched skin, hyper-defined hair strands, amplified fabric fibers, HDR-like processing or uniform sharpness.

Build the final prompt from the completed JSON: its `style` supplies the overall image direction; `character_dna`, `visual_translation_rules`, costume, body, environment, camera, lighting, quality, and negative prompt supply the non-negotiable constraints.

Before delivery, check for identity drift, changed hair or costume, inaccurate colors, added props, anatomy defects, incorrect camera, incorrect background, unwanted text, and watermarks.

Consistency Verification: compare the result against the current reference, `character_dna`, `immutable_anchor`, `uncertainty` and Transformation Delta. Check identity, face, hair, body, clothing, accessories, pose, color, materials, camera, composition and optical rendering. Also verify that facial expression and full-body pose are achievable through coordinated anatomy, muscles, balance and contact forces, and that hair shape follows its roots, material behavior, gravity, supported motion and collisions without losing Hair Design DNA. If a field drifts, strengthen only that field in the next generation or correction; do not rewrite or intensify the full prompt. Do not claim a check passed without inspecting the produced image. When only a prompt is returned, label verification as pending generation.

## Required priority and realism translation

Apply these layers in this fixed order. Later layers may translate the medium but may never override a higher-priority visible anchor: (1) original-artwork character DNA lock; (2) person identity lock; (3) composition and pose lock; (4) realistic human-anatomy translation; (5) realistic hair translation; (6) realistic clothing and material translation; (7) physical lighting lock; (8) real-camera imaging lock; (9) Anti-AI suppression. The current original artwork is the highest visual authority for every source-visible design fact except the explicitly fixed target body ratio `9 head body proportion`. The approved Step One JSON records and disambiguates visible facts; if it conflicts with the current artwork, preserve the artwork and flag the conflict rather than silently substituting JSON content. A current explicit user override affects only its named fields, as specified above. Do not fill any unseen, cropped, obscured or ambiguous region with invented design details.

Realism Translation Layer: preserve source-visible shape, color relationships, character identity and distinctive anatomy while converting drawn signs into plausible physical causes. Convert graphic contours into anatomy, depth, material boundaries and shadows only where the source supports them. Keep the fixed `9 head body proportion` target, facial relationships and pose; use joints, muscle, skin and perspective that could exist before a real camera. Do not flatten nonhuman design features into a generic human face or invent anatomy behind occlusion. Where a literal 2D geometry cannot physically exist, choose the smallest physically credible translation that retains its visible silhouette and identity; mark unresolved details unknown rather than redesigning them.

Hair Realism Translation: keep every source-supported hair DNA anchor in the existing Hair rule. Convert drawn clumps into plausible roots, strand groups, thickness, weight, gravity, flyaways and light response. A painted streak, cel-shaded band or graphic edge is design and lighting evidence, never automatic proof of dye, rigid hair geometry, a second hair color or a new accessory. Preserve the observed color distribution while letting a real light source produce natural highlights and translucency.

Physical Lighting Lock: retain the original image's visible light direction, relative intensity, color temperature, shadow placement, highlight placement and scene mood as far as they are supported. Translate painted highlights and shadows into light interacting with actual skin, hair, fabric and surroundings. Make cast shadows, contact shadows, ambient fill, reflections and falloff mutually plausible. Do not add a rim light, glow, multiple conflicting key lights, impossible reflections or new environmental light source to make the image more dramatic. If the source does not resolve a lighting parameter, leave it flexible within physically plausible limits.

Real-Camera Imaging Lock: preserve the source-supported viewpoint, framing, perspective, subject scale and depth relationships. Render as a photograph through a plausible lens with natural depth of field, focal-plane detail, optical falloff, restrained grain and credible exposure. Camera optics may explain perspective but may not move the camera or alter the locked pose and composition. Do not assert an exact focal length, camera brand, sensor, aperture or shutter setting unless the source or current user gives it. Respect the existing Natural Optical Rendering / Anti-Oversharpening rule.

2D design versus physical optics: when a drawn edge, shine, eye sparkle, skin gradient, hair highlight, fabric highlight or shadow conflicts with the locked real light/camera geometry, preserve the underlying design anchor and translate the mark into its nearest plausible material or optical effect. Do not reproduce cel-shading, flat color blocks, graphic outlines or painted reflections as literal marks on the photographed subject. If no physical reading is supported, omit the graphic rendering artifact while keeping the object's visible form and color hierarchy. Never use this conflict rule to change identity, garment construction, accessories, pose, camera or visible background.

Anti-AI Layer: aim for Extreme Photographic Realism + Physically Plausible Lighting + Real-Camera Imaging as the final hyperrealistic photographic result, including genuinely live-action facial skin. Do not use surreal or surrealistic to describe the target image. Suppress waxy or porcelain skin, uniformly perfect faces, repeated pores, over-smoothed texture, CG sheen, synthetic hair ribbons, wig-like clumps, floating fabric, weightless accessories, inconsistent hands or joints, fused details, impossible shadows, oversaturated color, HDR halos, uniform edge sharpness, text and watermarks. Keep natural asymmetry and material variation only within the source-visible character DNA; do not add freckles, scars, age marks or other new identity details merely to signal realism. The Anti-AI layer must never weaken higher-priority source locks.

At prompt assembly, state the positive target phrase exactly and carry the nine layers in order. Check 2D-to-physical conflicts before generation; after generation, inspect source fidelity and physical plausibility together. When a conflict cannot be resolved from the current image and JSON, say which detail is unknown instead of inventing it.
